## Who buys from this company

**Retail banking and regional credit unions** — They need to unify fragmented account data across physical branches and online channels while deploying AI agents to automate routine customer service cases.
  Best fit: A regional bank with 1,500 employees migrating away from legacy core databases to modernize digital support and cross-sell financial products.

**Industrial machinery and heavy equipment manufacturing** — They need to digitize complex sales pipelines and connect independent distributor networks to centralized order tracking and field service management.
  Best fit: A mid-market B2B equipment manufacturer with 400 employees selling through an international dealer network with disjointed lead-sharing processes.

**Omnichannel consumer goods and apparel retail** — They require a single source of truth for customer purchase history across physical and e-commerce storefronts to run personalized marketing and resolve high volumes of support requests.
  Best fit: A fast-growing retail brand with 600 employees operating dozens of brick-and-mortar stores alongside a high-volume direct-to-consumer web store.

**Private universities and higher education institutions** — They need an integrated system to track constituent relationships across the entire student lifecycle, from initial recruitment to automated campus helpdesk inquiries and alumni donation outreach.
  Best fit: A private university with 2,000 staff members and over 12,000 enrolled students managing operations across multiple decentralized colleges and administrative offices.
