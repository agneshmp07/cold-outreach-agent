## Who buys from this company

**Private Engineering and Technology Colleges** — They run dozens of high-volume campus recruitment drives each season and struggle to manage thousands of student eligibility profiles and shortlists across fragmented spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.
  Best fit: A NAAC-accredited private engineering college in Tier-2/3 cities with 800–1,500 graduating students managing 50+ visiting recruiters manually.

**Private Multidisciplinary Universities** — They need a single centralized system to coordinate concurrent placement drives across multiple departments without administrative overlap or communication bottlenecks.
  Best fit: A 10-year-old regional private university in South India with 2,500+ graduating students across engineering, management, and computer applications.

**Autonomous Business and Management Institutes** — They operate high-density placement seasons with complex multi-round interview schedules and require real-time tracking of offer statuses for lean placement teams.
  Best fit: An AICTE-approved autonomous management institute graduating 400 MBA students annually with a small two-person placement office.

**State-Affiliated Arts, Science, and Commerce Degree Colleges** — They are formalizing their placement cells to boost graduate employability metrics but lack institutional enterprise IT tools, relying entirely on manual Google Forms.
  Best fit: A high-enrollment affiliated degree college with 1,200 final-year undergraduates building out its first structured placement drives for regional service firms.
