## Who buys from this company

**Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) and Contact Centers** — They operate 24/7 multi-shift operations requiring safe, round-the-clock employee transit, strict safety compliance for late-night shifts, and automated multi-vendor cab billing.
  Best fit: A global BPO enterprise with 5,000+ employees operating 24/7 customer support centers across multiple delivery hubs in India or Southeast Asia.

**Global Capability Centers (GCCs) and Offshore R&D** — They run massive captive technology facilities for global parent companies and need automated AI routing and centralized fleet management to optimize high commute spending across thousands of engineers.
  Best fit: A US Fortune 500 retail corporation's offshore technology center employing 3,000+ staff on staggered shift schedules across multiple office towers.

**Commercial Banking and Financial Operations** — They maintain large back-office processing and market-clearing operations with late-night shifts that demand end-to-end live tracking, security escort integration, and complete audit compliance.
  Best fit: A multinational bank operating a centralized operational hub with 2,000+ back-office employees working rotational and late-night shifts.

**Healthcare Administrative and Revenue Cycle Services** — They rely on high-volume, round-the-clock physical office teams for medical coding and claims processing that depend on managed, automated shuttle and cab fleets to ensure shift punctuality.
  Best fit: A healthcare administrative services firm with 1,500+ night-shift medical coders operating out of a centralized campus.
