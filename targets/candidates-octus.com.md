## Who buys from this company

**Private credit and CLO asset managers** — They need continuous credit intelligence, covenant analysis, and debt tracking to underwrite new direct loans and monitor portfolio risk in sub-investment grade debt.
  Best fit: An alternative asset management firm with $500M+ AUM actively deploying capital into private credit funds or issuing Collateralized Loan Obligations (CLOs).

**Corporate law firms (restructuring and leveraged finance practices)** — Their attorneys require searchable credit document databases, precedent debt covenants, and court filings to structure complex debt agreements and represent clients in Chapter 11 proceedings.
  Best fit: An Am Law 200 firm with dedicated, multi-attorney bankruptcy, restructuring, or leveraged finance practice groups.

**Investment banks and debt capital markets advisory boutiques** — They need market intelligence and deal document comparables to structure leveraged buyouts, issue high-yield bonds, and advise corporate clients on liability management.
  Best fit: A mid-market investment bank with an active Leveraged Finance (LevFin) or Debt Advisory desk pitching debt issuances.

**Distressed debt and special situations hedge funds** — They rely on fast covenant analysis and legal docket tracking to spot mispriced default risks and trade non-investment grade corporate debt around bankruptcy events.
  Best fit: An event-driven or special situations fund actively purchasing defaulted corporate bonds or participating in debtor-in-possession (DIP) financing.
