## Who buys from this company

**B2B wholesale distributors and industrial suppliers** — They need an affordable, unified platform to connect sales pipelines, back-office accounting, and operational workflows without paying expensive per-seat software fees across large teams.
  Best fit: A 75-person regional industrial hardware distributor looking to replace fragmented spreadsheets and legacy desktop accounting software.

**IT service providers and managed service providers (MSPs)** — They require integrated CRM, HR, and operational management tools under a single license model to handle client retention and rapid employee scaling.
  Best fit: A 40-person managed service provider experiencing fast headcount growth and seeking to automate employee onboarding and sales pipeline tracking.

**Freight forwarding and third-party logistics (3PL) providers** — They need cloud-based business tools to streamline client communication, employee HR, and administrative workflows across distributed office and field teams.
  Best fit: A mid-sized logistics coordinator with 120 employees across three regional fulfillment centers seeking to unify scattered operational software.

**Multi-location outpatient clinics and dental groups** — They need structured HR onboarding, administrative management, and customer support tools on secure, privacy-focused cloud infrastructure that guarantees user data is never monetized.
  Best fit: A growing healthcare practice group with 15 locations needing to centralize HR processes and employee record-keeping across all sites.
