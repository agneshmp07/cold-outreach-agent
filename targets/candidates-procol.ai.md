## Who buys from this company

**Packaged food and personal care manufacturers** — They procure massive volumes of volatile raw ingredients and packaging materials from hundreds of vendors, requiring reverse auctions and ERP integration to protect gross margins.
  Best fit: A multi-factory packaged food enterprise with 1,000+ employees using SAP to source agricultural inputs and flexible packaging through frequent RFQs.

**Real estate developers and general construction contractors** — They process high volumes of decentralized purchase requests across multiple construction sites and need to reduce PR-to-PO cycle times while running transparent auctions for bulk materials like steel and cement.
  Best fit: A large-scale residential real estate developer with over 250 employees managing multiple concurrent township projects with site-level purchasing needs.

**Consumer electronics and home appliance manufacturers** — Sourcing multi-item bills of materials across large component supplier networks requires automated workflows and bidding tools to compress purchasing cycle times and lower direct costs.
  Best fit: An established electrical appliance manufacturer with 500+ employees expanding production lines and looking to automate component sourcing and invoice matching.

**Agrochemical and specialty chemical producers** — High-volume purchasing of chemical feedstocks and regional freight transport demands automated vendor onboarding, compliance tracking, and reverse auctions for transport logistics.
  Best fit: A multi-facility agrochemical company running an enterprise ERP system that procures bulk raw materials and manages complex regional transport routes.
