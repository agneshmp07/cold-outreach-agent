## Who buys from this company

**Consumer Electronics & Mobile Accessories Manufacturers** — They need rapid, high-volume direct-to-consumer distribution and turnkey fulfillment without building out their own global logistics network.
  Best fit: An emerging hardware brand with 20 employees launching a proprietary line of smartphone chargers and audio accessories.

**Shelf-Stable Packaged Food & Beverage Producers** — They require standardized nationwide shipping infrastructure and access to Prime subscribers to compete directly with national consumer packaged goods brands.
  Best fit: A regional specialty coffee roaster looking to expand nationwide without opening new physical distribution hubs.

**Apparel, Footwear, & Fashion Accessories Brands** — They leverage high buyer traffic and built-in advertising channels to clear seasonal inventory and acquire new customer segments efficiently.
  Best fit: A bootstrapped direct-to-consumer activewear brand scaling past two million dollars in annual revenue and seeking cross-border logistics.

**Independent Book Publishing Houses & Micro-Publishers** — They need print-on-demand distribution and digital Kindle marketplace access to eliminate upfront inventory printing and storage costs.
  Best fit: A small non-fiction publishing company managing a backlist of 40 titles seeking automated global order fulfillment.
