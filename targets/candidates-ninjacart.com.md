## Who buys from this company

**Independent Grocery Retailers and Supermarket Chains** — They need reliable daily store-door delivery of fresh fruits and vegetables to reduce procurement hassle, wastage, and wholesale market travel costs.
  Best fit: A multi-location neighborhood supermarket chain that sources fresh produce daily and wants to bypass traditional mandi middlemen.

**Hotels, Restaurants, and Commercial Catering (HoReCa)** — They require consistent, quality-graded bulk daily supplies of fresh ingredients alongside flexible trade credit to manage cash flow.
  Best fit: A regional casual dining chain or institutional caterer managing high-volume kitchen operations across a major city.

**Agri-Commodity Wholesale Traders** — They need scalable cross-regional fulfillment logistics, payment risk mitigation, and trade finance to execute larger produce trades.
  Best fit: A regional produce trader selling high volumes of seasonal fruits across state lines who lacks an in-house distribution network.

**Fresh Produce Exporters** — They need consistent bulk supply of export-grade produce with reliable quality control and trade financing support to meet international buyer commitments.
  Best fit: An agricultural export business shipping premium table grapes or onions to overseas retail markets.
