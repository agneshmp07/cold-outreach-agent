## Who buys from this company

**IT services and software engineering companies** — They need large-scale Grade-A IT park space with high-end infrastructure to accommodate growing engineering workforces and Global Capability Centers.
  Best fit: A US-based enterprise software firm establishing or expanding a 500+ seat offshore development center in Gurugram or Chennai.

**Apparel, luxury, and consumer retail chains** — They require prime storefront leases in high-footfall tier-1 shopping malls to expand their physical sales footprint and establish regional flagship stores.
  Best fit: A multinational fashion brand scaling its brick-and-mortar presence across major Indian metropolitan shopping centers.

**Banking and financial services institutions** — They need secure, prestigious commercial office spaces in established business districts for corporate headquarters and regional back-office hubs.
  Best fit: A private sector bank or wealth management institution consolidating city operations into a centralized corporate office in DLF CyberCity.

**Business process outsourcing and shared services centers** — They require high-density, 24/7 operational office spaces with uninterrupted power backup and robust transport connectivity for large shift-based teams.
  Best fit: A global BPO company opening a new 1,000-seat operational and customer service center in North India.
