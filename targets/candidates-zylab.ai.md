## Who buys from this company

**Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Apparel and Footwear Brands** — They constantly launch seasonal SKUs and require a continuous high volume of visual ad creative to prevent ad fatigue on social channels without traditional photoshoot costs.
  Best fit: A 10-employee online activewear brand running heavy Meta and TikTok ad campaigns that needs fast studio-style lifestyle photos and short promo videos for monthly collection drops.

**Cosmetics and Skincare Brands** — Their marketing heavily relies on high-quality visual proof, lifestyle imagery, and promotional video variations for fast-paced social media testing.
  Best fit: An e-commerce skincare startup with 5 to 25 employees scaling paid social ad spend while lacking a full-time in-house creative production team.

**Performance Marketing and Social Media Agencies** — They manage ad creative for multiple retail and D2C clients and need to rapidly generate and scale video and image ad variations to maintain client ROI.
  Best fit: A 10-person boutique digital agency specializing in paid social campaigns for a portfolio of Shopify-based consumer brands.

**Consumer Health and Nutrition Brands** — Frequent new product launches and bundle promotions demand fresh product packshots and promo video clips to maintain high conversion rates online.
  Best fit: A bootstrapped dietary supplement brand with under 20 employees that regularly launches new flavors and needs promotional video assets for digital marketing.
