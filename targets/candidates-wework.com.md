## Who buys from this company

**Growth-Stage B2B Software Companies** — They require immediate, turnkey office footprints in new regional markets to support rapid hiring without committing to long-term commercial leases.
  Best fit: A Series B software company with 150 employees expanding remote satellite hubs across three major tech cities.

**Enterprise Financial Services and Banking** — They need to reduce fixed capital expenditure on traditional real estate while providing flexible, fully managed regional hubs for a hybrid workforce.
  Best fit: A 2,000-person financial institution downsizing its centralized corporate headquarters in favor of regional hub-and-spoke offices.

**Management Consulting and Boutique Professional Services** — They need central, client-ready meeting spaces and office infrastructure without the operational burden of managing facilities, utilities, or long-term lease terms.
  Best fit: A 30-person strategy consultancy that frequently deploys temporary project teams in new metros for long-term client engagements.
