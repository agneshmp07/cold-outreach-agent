## Who buys from this company

**Global IT Services & Software Development** — They employ large, globally distributed remote workforces across multiple currency zones that require automated peer recognition, spot bonuses, and retention rewards.
  Best fit: A 2,500-person offshore software development consultancy with engineering hubs in Poland, India, and North America using Workday and Salesforce.

**Consumer Durables & Automotive Parts Manufacturing** — They rely heavily on multi-tiered indirect distribution networks and require automated software engines to pay out channel partner incentives, dealer SPIFFs, and sales rebates globally.
  Best fit: A mid-sized automotive aftermarket parts manufacturer distributing products through 5,000+ independent regional dealers and distributors worldwide.

**Retail Financial Services & FinTech Neobanks** — They need enterprise-compliant, instant multi-currency reward payouts to power customer acquisition, card-linked loyalty programs, and user referral campaigns.
  Best fit: A scale-up cross-border remittance or neobanking platform operating across Europe and Latin America running high-volume user referral incentive programs.

**Pharmaceutical & Medical Device Manufacturers** — They manage large regional field sales forces and third-party healthcare distribution reps who require compliant, automated commission tracking and reward fulfillment.
  Best fit: A medical equipment manufacturer with 1,200 employees and hundreds of independent sales agents distributed across multiple regional healthcare systems.
