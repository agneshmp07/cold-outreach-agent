## Who this company sells to

MoveInSync sells to large enterprise businesses with heavy office-based or multi-shift workforces that provide transportation for their employees. They target Facility Managers, Workplace Operations Heads, and Corporate Security Directors at organizations with over 1,000 employees. Good prospects manage vendor fleets, corporate cabs, or fixed-route shuttles across multiple office locations.


**This describes who PAYS:** Enterprise organizations that manage and fund commute transportation for their workforce.

_Business model: direct. Day-to-day users: Enterprise employees booking rides, drivers operating corporate cabs or shuttles, and workplace transport administrators.._

## Weakest assumption

Assuming enterprise companies in Western markets (US/Europe) offer company-provided commute cabs at the same volume as in India/LATAM/SE Asia. The human should verify whether the prospect actively manages employee cab fleets versus offering general public transit benefits.

## How to grade this

Before you use it, answer these four. They take five minutes and they are the difference between an ICP and a wish list.

1. Could you actually SEE every buying signal from outside? Cross out the ones you could not.
2. Is the does-not-fit table genuinely different from the inverse of the fits table? If not, the description of your product was too vague - rewrite it and re-run.
3. Does this ICP rule out any real company you were considering? If it rules out nobody, it is useless.
4. Do you agree with the weakest assumption it named? If not, name the one you think is actually weakest and write it in.
