## Who this company sells to

Salesforce sells to mid-market and enterprise businesses, educational institutions, and growing small businesses across industries like financial services, retail, manufacturing, and healthcare. Suitable prospects have dedicated sales or service operations, manage large volumes of customer interactions, and need to centralize disconnected systems into a unified platform. Good targets are looking to automate customer service, boost lead conversion, and integrate business workflows across departments.


**This describes who PAYS:** Business executives and technology leaders, such as CIOs, Chief Digital Officers, and heads of sales, service, or marketing.

_Business model: direct. Day-to-day users: Sales representatives, customer service agents, marketers, IT managers, and operational teams across an organization.._

## Weakest assumption

Assuming that mid-market SMB prospects are looking for advanced 'agentic AI' automation rather than basic CRM data organization; a cold outreach rep should verify if the target company already has basic CRM processes in place before pitching AI agents.

## How to grade this

Before you use it, answer these four. They take five minutes and they are the difference between an ICP and a wish list.

1. Could you actually SEE every buying signal from outside? Cross out the ones you could not.
2. Is the does-not-fit table genuinely different from the inverse of the fits table? If not, the description of your product was too vague - rewrite it and re-run.
3. Does this ICP rule out any real company you were considering? If it rules out nobody, it is useless.
4. Do you agree with the weakest assumption it named? If not, name the one you think is actually weakest and write it in.
