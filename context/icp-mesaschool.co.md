## Who this company sells to

Mesa targets venture-backed startups and growth-stage companies with 20 to 500 employees looking to hire for founder's office, product, and growth roles. A good prospect is an actively scaling Indian startup that values practical building experience over traditional MBA degrees. A bad prospect is a large, highly matrixed enterprise with rigid traditional hiring requirements.


**This describes who PAYS:** Individual students who pay tuition fees for the PGP program.

_Business model: marketplace. Day-to-day users: Students enrolled in the PGP program learning business, AI tools, and sales while building ventures.._

## Weakest assumption

Assumes partner startups pay a fee or enter formal recruitment contracts rather than participating informally for free access to candidate resumes. This can be verified by asking Mesa's corporate partnership team about their employer agreement structure.

## How to grade this

Before you use it, answer these four. They take five minutes and they are the difference between an ICP and a wish list.

1. Could you actually SEE every buying signal from outside? Cross out the ones you could not.
2. Is the does-not-fit table genuinely different from the inverse of the fits table? If not, the description of your product was too vague - rewrite it and re-run.
3. Does this ICP rule out any real company you were considering? If it rules out nobody, it is useless.
4. Do you agree with the weakest assumption it named? If not, name the one you think is actually weakest and write it in.
