ZyLab sells to e-commerce brands and marketing agencies with under 50 employees that need a continuous volume of visual marketing assets. Good prospects frequently launch products or run video ad campaigns on social channels and need studio-quality visuals without traditional production overhead. They buy either access to the AI platform or direct agency support.


**This describes who PAYS:** E-commerce brand owners, digital agency directors, and marketing leaders.

_Business model: direct. Day-to-day users: E-commerce managers, social media marketers, graphic designers, and content creators.._

## How to grade this

Before you use it, answer these four. They take five minutes and they are the difference between an ICP and a wish list.

1. Could you actually SEE every buying signal from outside? Cross out the ones you could not.
2. Is the does-not-fit table genuinely different from the inverse of the fits table? If not, the description of your product was too vague - rewrite it and re-run.
3. Does this ICP rule out any real company you were considering? If it rules out nobody, it is useless.
4. Do you agree with the weakest assumption it named? If not, name the one you think is actually weakest and write it in.
