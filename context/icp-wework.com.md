## Who this company sells to

WeWork sells flexible workspace solutions to mid-sized growing businesses, enterprise companies, and remote-first teams. They target Heads of Real Estate, Workplace Experience Managers, and Operations Directors who need adaptable office space without committing to long-term commercial leases. A strong prospect is a business undergoing geographic expansion or shifting to a hybrid work model.


**This describes who PAYS:** Company operations leaders, real estate heads, HR leaders, or individual business owners.

_Business model: direct. Day-to-day users: Employees, distributed teams, freelancers, and business professionals needing daily workspace or meeting facilities.._

## Weakest assumption

Assuming large enterprise buyers will choose flexible workspace providers over traditional direct lease renewals. This can be verified by checking whether target enterprise prospects are actively reducing their owned real estate footprint in public financial disclosures or news announcements.

## How to grade this

Before you use it, answer these four. They take five minutes and they are the difference between an ICP and a wish list.

1. Could you actually SEE every buying signal from outside? Cross out the ones you could not.
2. Is the does-not-fit table genuinely different from the inverse of the fits table? If not, the description of your product was too vague - rewrite it and re-run.
3. Does this ICP rule out any real company you were considering? If it rules out nobody, it is useless.
4. Do you agree with the weakest assumption it named? If not, name the one you think is actually weakest and write it in.
