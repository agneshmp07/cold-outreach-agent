## Who this company sells to

Xoxoday sells to mid-market and enterprise businesses with distributed workforces, multi-country sales teams, or large channel partner networks. Ideal target companies operate in multiple currencies and need to automate recognition, sales quotas, or loyalty rewards. They are good prospects if they are currently managing global incentive payouts through fragmented regional vendors or manual gift card processes.

## Weakest assumption

Assuming that enterprise buyers prefer buying a single platform for HR, sales, channel, and customer loyalty rather than dedicated point solutions for each department. A human can check this during cold outreach by asking prospects whether incentive budgets and vendor decisions are centralized or managed independently by each department.

## How to grade this

Before you use it, answer these four. They take five minutes and they are the difference between an ICP and a wish list.

1. Could you actually SEE every buying signal from outside? Cross out the ones you could not.
2. Is the does-not-fit table genuinely different from the inverse of the fits table? If not, the description of your product was too vague - rewrite it and re-run.
3. Does this ICP rule out any real company you were considering? If it rules out nobody, it is useless.
4. Do you agree with the weakest assumption it named? If not, name the one you think is actually weakest and write it in.
