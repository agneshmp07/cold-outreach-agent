# ICP - Amazon

> Drafted by profile.py from the company's own website. Read it and cut what is wrong before trusting it. A generated ICP that nobody graded is a guess with formatting.

## Who this company sells to

Amazon targets physical product brands, third-party merchants, and consumer goods manufacturers that want to sell products online. These businesses typically range from emerging consumer brands to established wholesale product companies with shippable inventory. A good prospect is a business with physical products suited for e-commerce distribution seeking a high-traffic sales channel and logistics support.


**This describes who PAYS:** Third-party merchants, product brands, publishers, and advertisers paying listing fees, transaction cuts, fulfillment fees, and ad placements.

_Business model: marketplace. Day-to-day users: Individual consumers shopping for physical goods, streaming digital content, or ordering household items.._

| Heavy Industrial Equipment Manufacturers | They build high-cost bespoke industrial machinery bought via sales teams rather than consumer add-to-cart checkout. |

## Weakest assumption

Assuming third-party merchants on the e-commerce store are the primary outreach target rather than prospective AWS cloud clients or enterprise procurement accounts on Amazon Business. A human should confirm which business unit (Seller Services vs. AWS vs. Amazon Business) outreach is intended for.

## How to grade this

Before you use it, answer these four. They take five minutes and they are the difference between an ICP and a wish list.

1. Could you actually SEE every buying signal from outside? Cross out the ones you could not.
2. Is the does-not-fit table genuinely different from the inverse of the fits table? If not, the description of your product was too vague - rewrite it and re-run.
3. Does this ICP rule out any real company you were considering? If it rules out nobody, it is useless.
4. Do you agree with the weakest assumption it named? If not, name the one you think is actually weakest and write it in.
