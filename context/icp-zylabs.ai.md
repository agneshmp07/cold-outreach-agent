## Who this company sells to

Zylabs sells to B2B technology companies, service providers, and agencies, such as SaaS, IT services, cybersecurity, and consulting firms. These businesses typically range from 10 to 500 employees and sell high-value solutions requiring consultative outreach. A good prospect is an organization with an existing outbound sales effort that struggles with low cold email response rates.


**This describes who PAYS:** Heads of Sales, VPs of Revenue, Chief Executive Officers, or Agency Owners.

_Business model: direct. Day-to-day users: Account Executives, SDRs, Founders, and sales teams running outbound campaigns.._

## Weakest assumption

The main assumption is that Zylabs fully automates trigger mapping and outreach message generation without requiring significant manual configuration by the seller. A human can verify this by requesting early access, submitting a website URL, and testing whether the system generates accurate triggers and usable outreach without heavy manual editing.

## How to grade this

Before you use it, answer these four. They take five minutes and they are the difference between an ICP and a wish list.

1. Could you actually SEE every buying signal from outside? Cross out the ones you could not.
2. Is the does-not-fit table genuinely different from the inverse of the fits table? If not, the description of your product was too vague - rewrite it and re-run.
3. Does this ICP rule out any real company you were considering? If it rules out nobody, it is useless.
4. Do you agree with the weakest assumption it named? If not, name the one you think is actually weakest and write it in.
