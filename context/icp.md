# MNGO ICP — company side
Last reviewed: 2026-09-09

## Who fits
| Industry | Company size | What must be true about their campus hiring | Why they would care |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IT Services & Consulting | 500–5,000 employees | Hires 100+ freshers annually across 5–20 tier-2/3 engineering colleges | Spreadsheets break at this volume of multi-college coordination |
| Enterprise SaaS & Product | 200–1,000 employees | Runs structured campus drives at 3–10 engineering colleges | Engineering leads and HR get pulled into scheduling chaos |

## Who does NOT fit
| Looks similar but is wrong | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Massive IT services (TCS, Infosys, Wipro) | Entrenched SAP/Workday or internal monoliths |
| Series A/B startups | Hire ad-hoc via referrals, not structured multi-college drives |
| GCCs with low intake | Tier-1 IIT/NIT referral pools through a standard ATS |
| Traditional manufacturing | Low-volume intake, handled by local walk-ins |
| Fintech and regulated financial services | Cut from the fits table. Their security and vendor-onboarding standards are a reason to refuse an unproven vendor, not a reason to buy. Hardest segment, not the third-best. |
| Companies at Razorpay's profile | Campuses chase them. No visible campus-hiring pain. |

## Buying signals — survived grading
| Signal | Where I see it from outside |
| --- | --- |
| Campus Recruiter / campus hiring job post that collects resumes via a Google Form or a personal email address | LinkedIn job posts, Naukri, company careers page |
| Candidate complaints about chaotic interview scheduling or delayed offers | AmbitionBox, Glassdoor. Score on it, never cite it in a message. |

## Signals cut, and why
- Active batch hiring campaign launch — visible, but true of every company that hires freshers. Signals that they hire, not that hiring is broken.
- Rapid expansion of the target college list — real pain, not publicly visible. Companies do not publish their college lists.

## Weakest assumption
Whether a company will engage before MNGO has signed colleges. The cheap test is the conditional ask: would you look at a shortlist if we brought you one. Costs them nothing, needs no vendor code, and a yes is what closes a college.

## Rule
No company name from this file goes into any prompt file. Prompts read this; they do not contain it.
