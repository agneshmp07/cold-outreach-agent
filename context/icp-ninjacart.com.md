## Who this company sells to

Ninjacart sells to fresh produce retailers, agricultural traders, and food wholesalers in India. These range from small local grocery shop owners to regional produce distribution businesses. A good prospect is a business dealing in fresh fruits and vegetables that deals with daily procurement inefficiencies, price volatility, or supply chain wastage.


**This describes who PAYS:** Fresh produce retailers, wholesalers, traders, importers, and exporters who buy produce or use fulfillment and credit services.

## Weakest assumption

Assuming that traditional small-scale fresh produce retailers are ready to shift from cash-based mandi buying to an app-based procurement model; test by checking local digital adoption rates and preferred payment terms in targeted cities.

## How to grade this

Before you use it, answer these four. They take five minutes and they are the difference between an ICP and a wish list.

1. Could you actually SEE every buying signal from outside? Cross out the ones you could not.
2. Is the does-not-fit table genuinely different from the inverse of the fits table? If not, the description of your product was too vague - rewrite it and re-run.
3. Does this ICP rule out any real company you were considering? If it rules out nobody, it is useless.
4. Do you agree with the weakest assumption it named? If not, name the one you think is actually weakest and write it in.
